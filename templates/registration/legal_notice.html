{% else %} Your information

Privacy Notice

This notice explains the information needed to create and operate a KERMS account and business workspace.

Information collected

Account registration collects your name (if provided), username, email address, password, workspace name and code, and the versions and time of your terms and notice acceptance. Passwords are stored using Django’s password hashing; they are not stored as plain text. You must verify your email before signing in.

Workspace information

KERMS stores business and risk information that workspace members enter, such as profiles, assessments, locations, risks, controls, and incidents. Workspace membership and role settings determine which signed-in members can access that organization’s information.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide account access and the requested workspace features. Verification emails are sent through the configured email service. If you use M-Pesa billing, payment details are sent to Safaricom’s Daraja service to process and verify your payment. Business-location maps may request map tiles from OpenStreetMap or the configured tile provider.

Security and retention

KERMS uses access controls and technical safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Information is retained as needed to operate the service, support the workspace, and meet applicable obligations. Workspace administrators control member access; do not enter sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to do so.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional profile information. For account corrections or privacy requests, contact your workspace administrator while the service operator’s designated privacy contact is being established.
