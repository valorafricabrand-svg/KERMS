A simple place to start

Risk management that fits the way you work.

Start with what you know about your business. Build a more useful picture over time, one practical step at a time.

01 / BUSINESS⌂

Describe your business

Capture your sector, customers, team, suppliers, sales channels, and the way your business operates.

02 / PLACES⌖

Map your operating sites

Keep shops, farms, market stalls, workshops, and other operating locations in one business view.

03 / ASSESS◎

Talk through real risks

Use repeatable check-ins and guided prompts to identify what could affect customers, cash flow, or operations.

04 / ACT↗

Turn priorities into action

Record risks, agree practical treatments, assign owners, and review progress as circumstances change.
